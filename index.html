THE EXPERIENCE BEHIND IKON

Senior finance experience. A personal approach.

Pankaj Jagota, FCCA

Founder & Managing Director | Accounting & Business Advisory

Pankaj is a Fellow Chartered Certified Accountant (FCCA) with over a decade of experience spanning corporate finance, M&A, and core, commercial and operational finance, operating at both individual business and group level.

His career spans privately owned and private equity-backed businesses, where he has played an active role in shaping financial strategy, strengthening business performance and supporting organisational growth. His experience extends to establishing and developing finance functions, building and mentoring teams, and contributing and leading cross-functional initiatives, including ERP implementations, finance transformation and business process improvements.

Pankaj also brings extensive experience in corporate transactions, having worked on both buy-side and sell-side M&A projects throughout his career, both as a consultant during his tenure at PwC and subsequently within corporate environments.

With an academic background in Economics, Banking and Finance and a Master’s in International Finance and Risk Management, he combines technical expertise with commercial judgement and practical leadership experience.

Alongside leading IKON, Pankaj holds a Strategy and Finance Director role in the industry, bringing a commercially focused and strategic perspective to the advice and support he provides to clients.

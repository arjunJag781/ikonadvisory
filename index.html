ABOUT IKON

Professional expertise. Personal understanding.

IKON Accounting & Advisory Limited is a UK-wide virtual accounting and business advisory practice, supporting individuals, self-employed professionals and small businesses across the United Kingdom.

We operate through a flexible, digitally connected approach, allowing us to work with clients wherever they are based in the UK. You do not need to be local to us to benefit from our support.

We believe good accounting is about more than just numbers. It is about giving people clarity, confidence and the support they need to make informed financial decisions.

Our approach is straightforward: we take the time to understand your circumstances, explain things in plain English and provide reliable, practical support tailored to your needs.

Whether you need help with your tax return, bookkeeping, VAT, payroll, cash flow or wider business planning, we aim to make the financial side of things simpler.
